FitMine Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

FitMine is operated by FitMine. It is a tool for fitness education, training plans, and workout records. This policy explains how FitMine handles your data. FitMine provides training information and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

1. Use without an account

You can use the main features without creating an account. When you use FitMine without signing in, your training plans, workout records, favorites, settings, body information, and assessment inputs remain on your device and are not uploaded through cloud sync.

2. Data processed when you sign in and sync

When you choose to create an account and sign in, cloud sync is enabled and FitMine processes:

  • Account information: your email address and a system-generated user ID.
  • Health and assessment information you choose to enter: age, physiological sex, body weight, heart rate, pre-exercise risk questionnaire responses, and cardio and strength assessment inputs and results.
  • Fitness information: training goals, plans, scheduled exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, completion status, and training trends.
  • App preferences and interaction data: language setting, favorite exercises, plan preferences, and sync status.
  • Security and technical data that may be generated by sign-in or API requests: IP address, browser or device user-agent, request time, and authentication event logs.

3. How we use data

We use this data to authenticate accounts, send verification codes, back up and restore data, sync across devices, generate and display personalized training content, protect the service, prevent abuse, and respond to support requests. FitMine does not sell personal data, display third-party ads, or use data to track you across apps or websites.

4. Service providers

FitMine uses Supabase for account authentication, database services, cloud functions, and security logs. It uses Resend to send sign-in verification emails. These providers process the data needed to deliver their services under their applicable data protection and security terms.

5. Retention and security

Cloud data is transmitted over HTTPS and retained while needed to provide account and sync features. Security logs, backups, and records that must be retained by law may remain for limited periods under the service providers' retention practices. FitMine uses access controls and row-level security to limit access to each user's data.

6. Your choices and rights

You can use FitMine without signing in. After signing in, you can sign out in Account & Sync, or use Delete account and cloud data to permanently delete your account and associated cloud training data. You can also email us to request access, correction, or deletion. See our Privacy Choices page for instructions.

7. Changes to this policy

If our features, service providers, or data practices change materially, we will update this policy and its effective date and notify you when appropriate.

8. Contact

Operator: FitMine. For questions about this policy, privacy requests, or product support, contact support@fitminetech.com.
