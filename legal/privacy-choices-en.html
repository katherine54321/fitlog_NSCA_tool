FitMine Privacy Choices

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Use FitMine on this device only

You can use the main features without signing in. While signed out, training plans, workout records, favorites, and assessment data are stored on your current device.

Sign out of your cloud account

In the app, open My → Account & Sync, then sign out. Signing out does not automatically delete data previously synced to the cloud.

Delete your account and cloud data

In the app, open My → Account & Sync → Delete account and cloud data, read the notice, and confirm. After successful deletion, your account and associated cloud training and assessment data will be deleted, and the app will return to the signed-out state.

Access, correction, and other requests

If you cannot complete an action in the app, or wish to request access, correction, deletion, or another privacy action, email support@fitminetech.com from the address used for your account. Do not send passwords or verification codes by email.
